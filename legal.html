Host

Name, address and phone number of the site’s hosting provider.

Your data

No cookies, no account, no visitor analytics, no advertising.

What stays on your device

The chosen place, your ratings, your spots, the sound setting and the latest weather are stored in your browser. They are not sent to anyone. To delete them, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

What is sent to Open-Meteo

For the weather and terrain, your browser contacts Open-Meteo directly with the coordinates of the chosen place, and those of your spots if you add any. The name of a city you search for is sent to it too. As with any website you visit, Open-Meteo then receives your IP address.

Location and compass

“Use my location” and “My location” only set the place or a spot, and your browser asks for your permission first. The phone’s orientation, used by the compass, never leaves your device.

Server logs

Like any host, the site’s hosting provider records technical connection data (IP address, date, requested page) to keep the service secure.

Your rights

The site keeps no data about you. For any question about your personal data, you can contact your national data protection authority.

Terms of use

The site is free and provided as is, without any warranty. The times are calculated, but the forecasts and the score remain estimates: don’t rely on them for your safety, for example when heading out at dusk.
